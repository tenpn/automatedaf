Looking at the unity security news this week, where several thousand developers found themselves suddenly responsible for updating years-old shipped projects, I felt a pang of anxiety on their behalf. How many of those projects have reproducible documented build pipelines, and how expensive will it be to QA those patches?

I’m not trying to be all “I told you so” - people make decisions with the information they had at the time - but I think publishers have a responsibility to help devs, especially if it is their first game, to understand best practices and how it doesn’t have to break the bank even if you’re a small team.

Lots of publishers already do this for things like loc, cert and QA. I don’t know why more don’t offer centralised build server/QA engineering resources!

If their source repo contained both build server scripts and some test automation, resurrecting an old game for a patch would not half as daunting.
