As I talk to studios about why they're yet to adopt test automation, I often hear misconceptions like "it's too expensive", "it'll slow everyone down", or "I'd rather get more features in before alpha". I am getting pretty good at countering those arguments, but figured it was time to record them in a way that's easier to share.

So I put together a talk on why automation works, for non-programmers, that came out at around 40 minutes, and hosted it on a new Automated AF YouTube channel: https://youtu.be/HbbE4DKGJb0

The people I showed it to thought it was great, but also thought if I email it to a sceptical producer, they're never going to spend 40 minutes watching it!

So I spent the extra time to condense it down to a sharp 10 minutes, where I introduce automation, talk about the concrete benefits it has, discuss how it can save you so much time it pays for itself, and a little shameless plug for my services at the end:
