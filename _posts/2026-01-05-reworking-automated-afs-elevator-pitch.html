I've been experimenting with taglines for my consultation work for a while. It turns out this is by far the hardest, least exciting, most difficult to quantify part of running a business!

This is extra confusing because what I do - "quality engineering and test automation" - doesn't mean a whole lot to a ton of people in the industry. And those that it does mean something to might have preconceptions about how useful a discipline it is!

So for a while I was going with "helping game developers of all sizes ship faster with pragmatic use of automated testing and studio devops", which is what you still see right now on https://andrewfray.dev.

However I've gone off this a bit, not least because it's a mouthful, but also it's not super specific. I've got a specific outcome in there ("shipping faster") but that's only one benefit, and I've still got the potentially-off putting "automated testing". The word "pragmatic" is important to me in my discipline, but do customers really care at this early stage? Probably not.

So, let's start the year by simplifying. Although I like "of all sizes" because I want to encourage studios who think they're too small for this kind of thing to see how wrong they are, better to spend that time talking more about the kinds of problems I can solve. So how about this:

Helping teams improve quality and ship faster

This doesn't cover how I solve those problems - build servers, test automation - but they are very common for a whole range of studios. I'm hoping this is a much clearer way to introduce what I do and start conversations.

What do you think? Reach out and let me know! I'm not 100% sure that works, but I guess we'll find out!

Happy testing!
