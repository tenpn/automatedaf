Quality engineering is as much about the people as it is the technology.

Recently I've been helping a client pare down their build server costs, which, because of changing circumstances, no longer fit their budgets.

I of course dived into their project code and got a handle on the flow of data, but the most important thing I did was talk to the different stakeholders, like design, art, QA, engineering and production, about how they use the current system, what their expectations are, and what their frustrations are.

That let me identify some savings that will not impact people's workflows too much, and as a bonus I discovered a few UX improvements that will be super quick to implement!

If you're running test automation or build servers at your studio, talk to your colleagues. They're your customers as much as your team mates, and you might learn some interesting things about how they're using your services.

Happy testing!
