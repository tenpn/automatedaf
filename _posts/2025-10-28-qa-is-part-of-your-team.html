I know I'm preaching to the choir on this blog, but this is important so I want to say it anyway:

QA is part of your team, and you should treat them like it.

The same way you'd focus on the workflow of your level designers so they can do their best work, you should focus on your QA and make sure they have all the tools they need to succeed:

- do they need any cheats or dev tools to test features efficiently?
- how easy is it for them to deal with filtering and attaching logs to bug reports?
- are they spending their day doing repetitive checklists that could be automated, instead of real exploratory work?

The best tooling makes you faster, which makes you better able to respond to the game and its needs. Ultimately, you don't just get "the same game but quicker", you get a better game more quickly.

And the exact same thing is true for QA and their workflows.

Reach out to your QA and find out what they need today!
